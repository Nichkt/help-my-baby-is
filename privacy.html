Privacy policy

Last updated November 28, 2026.

The short version

help! My baby is: does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

If you type your pediatrician's phone number into the app, it is saved only on your own phone or browser so the Nurse line button can call it. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it by clearing the app's storage.

Phone calls and links

Tapping a phone number opens your phone's dialer. Tapping a link to another website opens your browser. The app does not see or record either.

Fonts

When you are online, the app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Google may receive your device's IP address as part of that request, under Google's privacy policy. The app works fully offline without them.

Children

The app is written for parents and caregivers. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Medical information

The guidance in this app is general information for parents and does not replace advice from your child's doctor. If you think your child is in danger, call 911.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Nichkt/help-my-baby-is.
